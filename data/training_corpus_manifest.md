# Training corpus manifest

SHA-256 and byte size of every training corpus behind the released per-run outputs. The corpora are not tracked in git (`data/corpus/` is ignored); they regenerate from the listed commands, and the checksum lets a regeneration be checked against the file that was actually trained on. Checksums were computed on 2026-10-21 from the author's archived copies (`archive`) and, for the four large corpora, from the copies on the training host (`cloud`); where both exist they agree. The denoising pair matches the values quoted in `docs/mass_control.md`.

The held-out-grid corpora (`train_scale_*.txt`, 500k problems each, 32-shard generation) have their checksums in `results_cluster/data/*.sha256` and are regenerated by `scripts/gen_scale_corpora.sh`, `scripts/phase1_clean.sh`, `scripts/gen_learnable.sh`, `scripts/gen_learnable_mod.sh` and `scripts/gen_learnable_grad.sh`. The K-ratio corpora (`train_denoise_k{1,2,4}.txt`) are generated inside `scripts/phase_kratio.sh` (`generate_math_denoising.py --n 5000 --n-incorrect K --error-mode random --seed 42`); their files were not retained, only the per-run outputs. Evaluation inputs (paired test sets and corpus-level test streams) are in `data/eval_inputs_manifest.md`.

Known limitation: `data/generate_paired_test.py` at seed 999 no longer reproduces the committed paired test sets (generator drift noted in `TODO.md`); the training-corpus generators have not been re-run end to end against these checksums on a fresh machine, so a mismatch on regeneration should be reported rather than assumed to be a corruption of the archive.
